LibRedirect Instances List

This page fetches the LibRedirect instances JSON directly in your browser. No server-side processing — your client fetches the data on every visit. You can favorite services or URLs to pin them to the top of the list; favorites and network filter preferences are saved in your browser's local storage. You can clear only favorites or clear all localStorage (favorites + filter settings) using the buttons below. The "Check Availability" feature uses fetch() with no-cors mode, which returns opaque responses — instances that respond successfully are marked as reachable, but CORS-restricted instances may appear unreachable despite being online. Tor, I2P, and Loki instances cannot be checked from a regular browser and are automatically skipped.
