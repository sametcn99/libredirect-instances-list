About & Data Controls

This page fetches the LibRedirect instances JSON directly in your browser — no server-side processing. Favorites and filter preferences are saved in localStorage. Availability checking uses fetch() with no-cors mode; CORS-restricted instances may appear unreachable despite being online. Tor, I2P, and Loki instances are automatically skipped during checks.
